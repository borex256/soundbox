About Kim C

Kim C (Kim*C / Kim C UG) is a Ugandan recording artist known for Reggae, R&B and Dancehall. Known for songs like Kankyebeere, Kandye Ezange, Ndabirwamu ft Vinka and Mpulila from the Cassette project. Founder of Sub Sound Box Records.
